Surge League

8 VS 8 · ZERO GRAVITY · TWO GAMES · VERSION 5.1

A team sport for VR. Sixteen riders on jet boards fight over one glowing ball, the CORE, in zero gravity. You fly for BLUE with 7 teammates against 8 on ORANGE. There are two games, picked with the GAME tile on the menu. Station Rugby: get the CORE into their goals, first team to the target (7, 11 or 21) wins. Zone Control: no goals. Five rings, and the team with more riders inside a ring holds it and scores every second.

Press VR (bottom right). EARN YOUR PILOT LICENSE walks you through every control in about 3 minutes and turns your board gold. HOW TO PLAY is five short pages with a map of the level. Stuck on a training step? Pause, then SKIP THIS STEP.

EIGHT LEVELS

Station Rugby: Meridian Station, Neon Docks, Foundry Works, The Backrooms, The Mirror Vault and The Endless Chasm, a twelve-sided shaft with no floor and no ceiling: fly up or down forever. Two of its walls are false. Zone Control: Sky Gardens (no walls, floating gardens, two rings on lifts) and The Carousel (a round hall, two rings riding platforms that circle it).

On the space levels the windows are open: fly out and back in anywhere. Veined see-through walls are solid. Rocks and moving platforms are solid too. The CORE shows through every wall.

ZONE CONTROL

Fly into a ring and stay. While your team has more riders inside than the other, the ring fills with your color and then pays a point every 2 seconds. The rider carrying the CORE counts as 3. Hold all five rings and your points double. Dash into rivals to knock them out of a ring.

SCORING

1A lobby hole: the round holes in the wall at the other team's end.
3The deep goal: the long way round, behind, under or above their end.
+1Bank shot: bounce it off a wall on the way in.
+1Long shot: score from 28 m or further.
2Penalty: a free shot after the other team's late hit.

CONTACT

Ruck: fly into the rival carrying the CORE and you lock up. Every trigger and grip you squeeze adds strength, fluttering the triggers strips it, and teammates within 6 m bind on. Dash (B) into it for a head start.

Fight: punch a rival who isn't carrying. You both have a health bar. Their jabs flash red: block with a glove in front of your face, or move your head. Heavy swings flash gold: only a dodge saves you. After two clean hits they cover up, so hit when the guard drops, or right after you block or dodge (a counter hits much harder). A knockout sends the loser to the penalty box for 15 seconds. In Station Rugby, fight far from the CORE and you both sit. You can turn fights off on the menu.

Aurora shards drift through the level: fly through one and your whole team is faster and stronger for 7 seconds.

Controls

Quest: point your hands where you want to fly and push the left stick forward. Click the left stick to boost. Flick the right stick sideways to snap turn, up and down to rise and sink, and hold it down for the rear-view mirror. A turns you around. B dashes the way the left stick points. X calls for a pass. Point at the CORE and hold a trigger to pull it in. Carrying: tap the trigger for a soft throw, hold it for a hard one, let go while pointing at a teammate to pass. Grips snatch the CORE out of a rival's hands, and squeezing them while you carry makes you harder to rob. Y pauses. You always stay upright.

PC: WASD fly, E / Q up and down, drag to look, Shift boost, R dash, L turn around, hold G to pull the CORE, Space throws (hold to charge), F passes, C calls for a pass, V mirror, X punch, P or Esc pauses, Tab shows the score.
