Surge League version 2: dream realms

Two against two in the sky. Every match is a new dream realm with its own world, ride and power. Press the VR button, then use the menu in front of you.
Controls
Quest: left stick flies where you look, right stick rises, sinks and snap turns. A or X boosts, B uses the realm power. Grip grabs the disc or an air pocket; swing and let go to throw, flick your wrist to curve. Point at your ally and press trigger to pass. Steal from the front or side. Tap Y: Full motion / Easy reach. Hold Y: back to the menu. Click either stick: pause (resume, restart, change mode, switch to version 1, or exit to the home screen).
PC: WASD fly, E / Q up / down, drag to look, Shift boost, C power, hold G to pull the disc, Space throws, F passes. Arrow keys and Enter work the menu, R goes back to it, P or Esc pauses.
