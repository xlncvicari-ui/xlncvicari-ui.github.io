Surge League version 1 (classic)

Two against two in a round sky arena: you and your ally (blue) against two orange bots. Throw the disc through the orange hoops at the far end and guard your blue ones. Side hoops are worth 2, the tall middle one 1. Steals only work from the front or side. Catch the comet for 3. Matches last 4 minutes.
Quest: flying
Left stick: fly where you look
Right stick up/down: rise, sink
Right stick sideways: snap turn
A or X: boost
Grip an air pocket: hold on, pull, let go to fling
A: start, hold B: restart, Y: mode
Click a stick: pause menu (resume, restart, version 2, home screen)
Quest: your long arms
Reach out: arms stretch far
Grip near the disc: it snaps to your hand
Swing and let go: throw
Trigger: push the disc from afar
Easy reach (auto if you move little): point, hold trigger to pull, trigger again to throw
PC
WASD: fly where you look, E / Q: up / down, drag mouse: look, Shift: boost
Hold G while looking at the disc: pull it in. Space: throw where you look
Enter: start, R: restart, P or Esc: pause menu
